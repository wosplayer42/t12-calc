Terms used on this page

Troop type
One of Infantry, Lancer, or Marksman. Also called a camp. Costs scale by how many troop types you complete.
Exalted Gear
The five gear slots that unlock T12 research for a troop type (each maxed at Lv 5).
Molten I Tier
The four Molten I combat stats (Attack, Lethality, Health, Defense) for a troop type.
Troop Skill
The unique skill for that troop type (max Lv 3). Needs matching Molten I floors (Lv 5 / 10 / 15).
Ideal Joiner
One troop type fully prepared as a joiner: Exalted Gear max + all Molten I → 15 + Troop Skill Lv 3.
Support tracks
Exalted Training, Exalted Healing, and Exalted First Aid. Each track goes to Lv 10. A support package = all three tracks for one troop type.
