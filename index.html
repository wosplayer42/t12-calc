Before you start T12

If you cannot complete Troop Skill level 1, do not start T12 / Exalted research yet.

  • Troop Skill Lv 1 (Indomitable Wall / Meridian Phalanx / Starfire) is the first real power pay-off for joiners.
  • It needs all four Skills I nodes at Lv 5 plus full Tier 1 unlock (5 gear slots × Lv 5).
  • If you cannot afford that plan, keep farming crystals / steel / RSS first — partial T12 unlock with no skill is a weak use of resources.
  • Use the Troop Skill Lv 1 quick target to see the full cost of that milestone.
